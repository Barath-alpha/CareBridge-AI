Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started with CareBridge AI.

What file formats can I upload for my discharge summary?

CareBridge AI supports high-resolution photos (JPG, PNG) taken directly on your smartphone, scanned documents, and multi-page PDF files from hospital portals. Our OCR parser accurately handles both printed records and clear physician handwriting.

Is my medical information secure and private?

Yes. CareBridge AI adheres to strict HIPAA guidelines. All medical records and chat conversations are encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit via TLS 1.3. Your personal health information is never sold or used for third-party advertising.

Which languages does the AI assistant support?

CareBridge AI supports English, Hindi (हिंदी), Bengali (বাংলা), Tamil (தமிழ்), Telugu (తెలుగు), Spanish (Español), French (Français), and German (Deutsch), allowing patients and families to converse naturally in their primary language.

How do caregiver alerts work?

During setup, you can add phone numbers or email addresses of family members and caregivers. If you miss a vital medication dose or report high pain/fever symptoms during your daily check-in, the system automatically dispatches an instant WhatsApp/SMS notification.
